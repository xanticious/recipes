# Recipe ideas

Recipes that would make the first week easier for someone newly adjusting how they eat. None of these are in the catalog yet. They are suggestions for later, not a promise.

The current book is about fifty dishes. Some tags already have a comfortable set of starters. A few patterns are thin on purpose — we would rather wait than stretch a tag.

## Carnivore

The catalog currently has little that is only animal foods, salt, pepper, and water. Soft scrambled eggs qualify. Almost every other dinner includes a vegetable, an oil, or a sauce.

- Pan-seared steak with salt, finished in butter
- Bacon and eggs (no toast, no potatoes)
- Salmon baked in butter, no lemon or herbs if those fall outside the tag
- Bone broth as a simple sip or a first meal
- Roast beef or a small prime rib for a special occasion
- Pork chops, salt and pepper only
- Baked chicken thighs with salt, no vegetables on the pan

## Keto

Eggs, salmon, and a bunless burger already help. We are short on sides and anything sweet.

- Egg muffins with cheese and a tolerated vegetable
- Zucchini boats or zucchini noodles with a meat sauce (no sugar)
- Chicken thighs with green beans and butter
- A bunless smash burger platter (close to skillet burgers, without tomato if needed)
- A keto-friendly chocolate pot or cocoa fat bomb, if we want one dessert

## Paleo

Roasted chicken, salmon, and tuna boats cover dinner and lunch. Breakfast and dessert are thinner once grains and dairy are out.

- Egg and berry plate, no yogurt
- Sweet potato breakfast hash with chicken or bacon (if we treat sweet potato as paleo here)
- Grilled shrimp with lemon and olive oil
- A fruit-and-maple crisp without flour or butter
- Coconut-aminos stir-fry that never starts from soy sauce

## Vegan

Dal, rice and beans, chickpea curry, and hummus already exist. Breakfast is the gap — most of our mornings use eggs or dairy.

- Tofu scramble with turmeric and vegetables
- Overnight gluten-free oats with maple and berries (no yogurt)
- Coconut yogurt bowl
- A vegetable soup that uses vegetable broth from the start
- Veggie fried rice without the egg (or with a listed omit)

## Lactose-free

Plenty of savory meals already work. Creamy and baked things are harder.

- Lactose-free macaroni with gluten-free pasta
- A coconut-milk chocolate pudding
- Lactose-free yogurt berry bowl (we have a dairy yogurt bowl today)
- A grilled cheese that starts from lactose-free slices

## Low fructan / no onion, no garlic

We already have several honest no-allium meals. The popular pots still assume onion and garlic.

- Weeknight chili with no onion or garlic, using cumin, paprika, and chives
- Taco filling seasoned without onion or garlic powder
- Marinara that starts from garlic-infused oil or from tomato, basil, and salt only
- Dal or curry that uses ginger, turmeric, and garlic-infused oil
- A roast chicken gravy from garlic-free broth

## Low GOS

Most of the book is already fine. The missing piece is a satisfying vegetarian dinner that is not beans.

- Eggplant or zucchini bake with tomato and herbs
- A baked potato bar with tolerated toppings
- Tofu and rice bowl (if the tofu portion fits the person)

## Low fructose

Savory food is in good shape. Sweets often lean on honey, apples, or brown sugar.

- Berry crisp sweetened with maple
- Citrus and mint salad
- A chocolate dessert that uses white sugar or maple, not honey or molasses

## Low sorbitol / low mannitol / low polyol

We need snacks and sides that do not reach for apple, avocado, celery, mushrooms, or cauliflower.

- Cucumber spears with salt and olive oil
- Carrot coins roasted with paprika
- A green-bean and potato salad without celery
- Turkey roll-ups (no avocado)

## Low FODMAP and Low FOP

The first week is easier with more “boring on purpose” meals that already clear the cluster.

- Baked chicken thighs, rice, and zucchini
- Egg cups with chives and spinach
- White fish with rice and carrot
- A lactose-optional rice porridge (we have ginger rice porridge; a breakfast egg version would help)
- Snack plate: popcorn or rice cakes, cucumber, and a hard cheese if lactose is allowed (Low FOP)

## Breakfast variety

Mornings are where new limits feel loneliest.

- Omelette with chives, no onion
- Cottage-cheese bowl with a lactose-free swap
- Breakfast sausage and eggs (carnivore / keto)
- Smoothie that uses lactose-free milk and berries, not apple juice or honey

## How to use this list

When adding one of these, give it structured ingredients so tags derive from the catalog. If a popular dish can become safe with a listed swap, prefer a substitution group over a second almost-identical recipe.

Write the recipe the way we write the guides: assume the cook is tired, newly restricted, and hoping dinner can be ordinary again.
