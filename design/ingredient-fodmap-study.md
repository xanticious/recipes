# Ingredient FODMAP study

One entry per catalog ingredient. Classifications follow public Monash University FODMAP guidance and dietitian summaries (FODMAP Everyday, Kate Scarlata, A Little Bit Yummy). Many processed or uncommon foods are not lab-tested under that exact name; those are inferred from typical ingredients or a close tested relative. Serving amounts are approximate and can change as Monash updates the app.

Status is Low Fodmap, High Fodmap, or Depends on serving size. High and Depends entries name the FODMAP involved (fructans, GOS, lactose, excess fructose, sorbitol, mannitol).
