# Dinners

Home-eaten plates inspired by Italy INRAN-SCAI / IV SCAI, adults, everyday fare mostly at home.
Each line is a typical sitting: foods eaten together at one time and occasion.
Similar foods are stacked (toast with eggs counts with other egg-and-toast plates).
Frequency bands are qualitative (**Very common** / **Common** / **Also seen**).
Sitting counts were not computed from microdata.
